# Local SEO Visibility Guide (No Code Changes)

Since your website `sunauragroup.com` is live, follow these steps to make it appear on Google for keywords like **"SunAura"**, **"SunAura Group"**, and **"Geyser in Ranchi"** without touching the code.

## 1. Create a Google Business Profile (Critical)
This is the **#1 way** to appear for "in Ranchi" searches. It places you on Google Maps and the "Local Pack" (the map results at the top of search).

1.  Go to [Google Business Profile](https://www.google.com/business/).
2.  Sign in with your business Gmail.
3.  Enter your business name: **SunAura Group**.
4.  Category: **Solar Hot Water System Supplier** or **Heating Equipment Supplier**.
5.  Address: **8th Lane, Sarweshwari Nagar, Bajra, Itki Road, Ranchi, Jharkhand 834001**.
6.  Add your website: `https://sunauragroup.com`.
7.  Add your phone: `+91-9204418515`.
8.  **Complete Verification**: Google will usually send a postcard or ask for a video to verify you are at that location.

## 2. Submit to Google Search Console
This tells Google directly that your site exists so they index it faster.

1.  Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console/).
2.  Add a **URL Prefix** property: `https://sunauragroup.com`.
3.  **Verify Ownership**: Since you use AWS Amplify, the easiest way is to add a TXT record to your DNS (Spaceship) if prompted.
4.  **Submit Sitemap**:
    - In the left menu, click **Sitemaps**.
    - Type `sitemap.xml` in the "Add a new sitemap" box and click **Submit**.

## 3. List on Local Directories
Google looks for "citations" (your business name/address/phone on other sites) to trust you.

1.  **Justdial**: Create a free listing for "SunAura Group" in Ranchi.
2.  **IndiaMART**: Create a profile as a dealer for "Racold Geysers".
3.  **Sulekha**: List your business in the "Water Heater Dealers" category for Ranchi.
4.  **Google Maps Review**: Ask 5-10 happy customers to leave a review on your Google Business Profile with keywords like "Best geyser in Ranchi" or "Racold solar heater".

## 4. Social Media Linking
1.  **Facebook/Instagram Page**: Create a page for "SunAura Group".
2.  Put `https://sunauragroup.com` in the **Bio** or **Website** field.
3.  Post at least 1-2 photos of your installations (like the NIT Jamshedpur or Clarks Inn projects) and link back to your site.

## 5. Wait for Indexing
- Google takes time to "crawl" new websites.
- By doing the steps above, you should see your site starting to appear within **1 to 2 weeks**.
